# Global Action Button
## Overview
The Global Action Button (GAB) is a prominent and easily accessible component designed to highlight critical, frequent, or high-value actions within a specific context, primarily focusing on client engagements. It provides users with a direct pathway to essential functionalities.

## Usage Guidelines
The Global Action Button should be strategically employed to enhance user efficiency and engagement with core platform actions.

### When to Use
*   **High-Value Actions**: Implement the GAB for actions that carry significant importance or impact within a user's workflow.
*   **High-Frequency Actions**: Reserve the GAB for actions that users perform repeatedly, ensuring quick access and minimizing navigation steps.
*   **Client-Specific Engagements**: Ensure the actions are directly relevant and specific to managing or interacting with client engagements.

## Best Practices

### Do's
*   **Focus on Core Actions**: Add actions that are fundamental to client engagements and provide clear value.
*   **Maintain Action Integrity**: Ensure that the actions provided through the GAB are distinct and do not overlap redundantly with other interface elements.

### Don'ts
*   **Avoid Dilution**: Do not add actions that are low-value, infrequent, or can be easily accessed elsewhere, as this diminishes the perceived importance and utility of the GAB.
*   **Prevent Overload**: Avoid cluttering the GAB with too many options, which can lead to decision fatigue and reduced discoverability of truly important actions.
